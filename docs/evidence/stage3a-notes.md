## Pass 2 (reviewed head 709f88f): fail

- Verifier pass 2: fail, two DEFECTS, both about documentation, none about the code. (1) medium: HLD section 13 says a client at the minimum rate holds a slot at most about 400 seconds and describes the residual attack as below-rate accounts active about 12 seconds in every 72. The code allows more: a client may send X bytes quickly and then one byte just under every 30 seconds; the rate predicate runs only after a message, so such a client is cut about 410 seconds after a 25 MiB burst plus up to 29 seconds (435 s, about 441 s with the multipart allowance). Four verified companies that each send at or just above the minimum rate, or use burst-and-drip timing, can occupy all four process slots for roughly 410 to 441 seconds, end with invalid content so that no quota is used and no cooldown starts, and repeat; other companies then see 503. The behaviour is allowed by design, the stated bound and residual-risk text are not truthful. (2) low: README still says at most two uploads run and a third is busy.
